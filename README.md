
# Day Architect -- Schedule Builder

A daily schedule builder that takes your tasks, organizes them by priority, and generates a time-blocked plan for your day.

## How to Use

1. Type a task name, set the duration and priority level, then click **Add Task**
2. Add as many tasks as you need
3. Set your preferred start time on the right panel
4. Click **Generate Schedule** to build your day (or press `Ctrl+G`)
5. Your schedule appears as color-coded time blocks sorted by priority
6. Click the clipboard icon to copy your schedule as text (or press `Ctrl+E`)
7. Toggle light/dark mode with the theme button
8. Hit **Clear All** to reset and start over

Tasks and your schedule are saved automatically between sessions.
